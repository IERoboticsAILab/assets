CPL / IE LOGO ASSET PACK
Version 1 — 24 October 2026

This pack uses the approved circular circuit logo and its final, slightly spaced CPL lettering.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
• 6 fully vector SVG masters
• 24 high-resolution PNGs: each variant at 1024, 2048, 4096 and 8192 pixels square
• A preview sheet
• The approved 1254-pixel PNG reference
• The original supplied CPL typography SVG

VARIANTS
primary-white: black-and-white badge on a solid white square.
primary-transparent: black badge with white interior details; only the exterior is transparent.
reversed-black: white-and-black badge on a solid black square.
reversed-transparent: white badge with black interior details; only the exterior is transparent.
single-ink-black: black artwork with all negative spaces transparent. Best on a light background.
single-ink-white: white artwork with all negative spaces transparent. Best on a dark background.

WHICH FILE TO USE
SVG: preferred for print, signs, design software and any size that needs to scale. All artwork is vector, with no linked images or font dependencies.
PNG 1024: everyday digital use.
PNG 2048: presentations and documents.
PNG 4096: large digital placements and print layouts.
PNG 8192: highest-resolution raster export.

All PNGs have 300 pixels-per-inch metadata. The pixel dimensions determine their actual detail. At 300 ppi, an 8192-pixel canvas is approximately 27.3 inches square. Use SVG when the printer accepts it.

TRANSPARENCY
The primary-transparent and reversed-transparent versions retain solid interior colors so the badge reads consistently over another background.
The single-ink versions use one solid ink color. Their internal white/black areas are cut out and reveal the background beneath.
The preview sheet uses a checkerboard to show transparency; it is not part of the logo files.

DESIGN AND SOURCE
Colors: pure black (#000000) and pure white (#FFFFFF).
The square canvas, margins, monogram, circuit motif and approved CPL spacing are consistent across the pack.
The supplied CPL outline paths are retained exactly, with their approved uniform scale and placement along the curve.
The remaining emblem was faithfully reconstructed as smooth vector outlines from the approved raster artwork. The original approved PNG is included under Reference for comparison.

FOLDERS
SVG/ — vector masters
PNG/ — raster exports, grouped by variant
Reference/ — approved PNG and supplied CPL typography SVG
